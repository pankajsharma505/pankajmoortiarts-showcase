About Us

Crafting divine statues & pure marble sculptures

WALCOME TO PANKAJ MOORTI ARTS WEB PAGE.

With years of traditional craftsmanship passed down through generations, our master artisans bring stone to life. From fine facial expressions and intricate carving to durable finishing, every statue is crafted with devotion and perfection.

Whether you need a small deity idol for your home shrine, a massive sculpture for a temple, or custom artwork, we deliver high-quality marble masterpieces directly from our workshop to your doorstep worldwide.

Humare liye marble ki moorti banana sirf ek vyavasay nahi, balki ek pavitra bhakti aur kala hai. PICHLE kai varshon se, hum shuddh Makrana marble aur hamare bharat desh ki paramparik karigari ke saath bhavya bhagwan ki moortiyan aur custom sculptures tayar kar rahe hain.

Pankaj Moorti Arts ka mukhya uddeshya har ek devotee (bhakt) tak sarvochha gunvatta (best quality), bareek karigari, aur shuddh marble moorti pahuanchana hai. Aapke vishwas aur santushti hi humari sabse badi pehchan hai.

— Khyali Ram Sharma

Founder & Owner, Pankaj Moorti Arts
